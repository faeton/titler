# Close friends forces private APIs on n5

Titler can fully automate public video Stories on both platforms through official APIs. Close Friends audiences cannot be automated that way. On Instagram, the Graph API's `media_type=STORIES` publishes a 3–60 s video Story to a professional account (Business is the safe type) with no App Review. It has **no audience parameter, so every API Story is public**. Personal accounts have no official publishing path at all. Close Friends can only be reached in two ways: tapping "Close Friends" by hand in the Instagram composer, or the unofficial **instagrapi** library (`extra_data={"audience": "besties"}`). instagrapi releases almost daily, but its login flow has been shaky since September 2026. On Telegram, the Bot API's `postStory` works only for a Telegram Business account and also has no audience field. The MTProto user API (`stories.sendStory` with `privacy_rules`) supports close friends, contacts and selected users, and Telegram permits it with your own `api_id`. **Kurigram** is the best-maintained Python wrapper for it. The binding constraint on Telegram is quota, not tooling: **free accounts get 1 active story and 3 per week**, so a multi-part ~59 s series in practice needs Premium. Recommendation for Titler: add a "publish" stage on n5 with two adapters, Kurigram/MTProto for Telegram and instagrapi for Instagram Close Friends. Keep the official Graph API as a fallback for public posts, and keep a manual phone handoff as the escape hatch.

## The Instagram API publishes public Stories only, and only for professional accounts

The official route is documented and fits the clip format. A Story is created with `POST /<IG_ID>/media` using `media_type=STORIES` plus a `video_url`, polled until `status_code=FINISHED`, then published via `media_publish` ([Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing/)). The reference spec allows **3–60 s, max 100 MB**, MP4/MOV with the moov atom at the front, H.264 or HEVC, 23–60 fps, at most 25 Mbps, and AAC at 48 kHz/128 kbps ([Meta: IG User Media reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media)). A 59 s 1080×1920 H.264 faststart MP4 fits. The `video_url` must be publicly fetchable, and Titler's existing signed-link gateway already provides that. An app that "only serves your Instagram professional account" needs only Standard Access, with no App Review or Business Verification ([Meta: Instagram Platform overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/overview)). The rate limit is **100 API-published posts per rolling 24 h**, although the same page also says 50 in its carousel section ([Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing/)). Either figure is far above a few Stories a day.

There are three hard limits. First, **there is no audience or Close Friends field** anywhere in the content-publishing guide, the media reference or the Sharing-to-Stories docs. Stickers (link, poll, location), collaborators and alt text are also unsupported for Stories ([Meta: IG User Media reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media)). Second, **personal accounts are out**. The read-only Basic Display API shut down on 4 December 2024 ([Meta Dev Community](https://developers.secure.facebook.com/community/threads/1089913112732648)), and its replacement serves only professional accounts. Third, the docs don't settle **Creator vs Business**. Meta names no restriction, but a developer got `(#10) The user is not an Instagram Business` creating a Story container on a Creator account, and it worked after switching to Business ([Meta Dev Community](https://developers.secure.facebook.com/community/threads/1226500261819047)). Publer says "only Business accounts support direct Instagram Stories" ([Publer](https://publer.com/help/en/article/how-to-directly-schedule-instagram-stories-oij7gi/)). Buffer and SocialBee claim Creator works ([Buffer](https://support.buffer.com/articles/scheduling-instagram-posts-reels-stories-and-notifications-3XA98S9Q5p); [SocialBee](https://help.socialbee.com/hc/en-us/articles/29979116809623)). The likeliest reading is that the restriction applies to the older Facebook Login path and may have been relaxed on Instagram Login. The fix is simple either way: switching to Business is free and reversible.

Schedulers don't change this. Buffer, Publer and others wrap the same API, so they auto-post plain Stories and fall back to a phone push notification for anything with stickers, music or text. **None documents Close Friends**, because the API doesn't expose it ([Buffer](https://support.buffer.com/articles/scheduling-instagram-posts-reels-stories-and-notifications-3XA98S9Q5p)).

### The official phone handoff can't carry a 59 s clip

Meta's "Sharing to Stories" handoff (`instagram-stories://share` on iOS with a pasteboard payload, `com.instagram.share.ADD_TO_STORY` on Android) opens the normal Story composer, where Close Friends can presumably be tapped. It requires a Facebook App ID and a native app, and it caps the background video at **20 seconds** ([Meta: Sharing to Stories](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/sharing-to-stories/)). A web page or PWA can't write the custom pasteboard types. The web alternative is Safari's `navigator.share({files})`, which supports video over HTTPS from a user tap ([web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/web-share)). It hands the file to Instagram's share extension, but no current source confirms that the extension offers a Story destination. That needs an on-device test before Titler relies on it.

## instagrapi is the only live open-source path to Instagram Close Friends

Among unofficial libraries, only **subzeroid/instagrapi** (Python) is both maintained and complete. The repo was pushed on 2026-10-26, 3.2.1 shipped on 2026-10-25, and five releases landed in the preceding eleven days ([GitHub releases](https://github.com/subzeroid/instagrapi/releases); [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/instagrapi/)). It provides `video_upload_to_story(path, ..., extra_data={}, resize_mode="fill")`, and its docs list `extra_data={"audience": "besties"}` as the way to publish to Close Friends ([story.md](https://github.com/subzeroid/instagrapi/blob/master/docs/usage-guide/story.md)). A user confirmed that works in 2023 ([issue #1210](https://github.com/subzeroid/instagrapi/issues/1210)). The list itself can be managed with `close_friend_add` / `close_friend_remove` ([user.md](https://github.com/subzeroid/instagrapi/blob/master/docs/usage-guide/user.md)). The async twin **aiograpi** is equally active ([GitHub](https://github.com/subzeroid/aiograpi)). Version 3.2.0 requires **Python 3.11+** ([CHANGELOG](https://github.com/subzeroid/instagrapi/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md)).

The alternatives are dead or don't fit. **dilame/instagram-private-api** (Node) supports `toBesties: true`, but the public repo was last pushed in August 2024, hardcodes app version 222, and moved its maintained version behind a paywall ([GitHub](https://github.com/dilame/instagram-private-api)). ping/instagram_private_api last released in 2019 ([GitHub](https://github.com/ping/instagram_private_api)). Instaloader only downloads ([GitHub](https://github.com/instaloader/instaloader)). The paid HikerAPI is **read-only** and "never acts on an account" ([hikerapi.com](https://hikerapi.com/)). No maintained open-source "scheduled Stories" project exists ([GitHub search](https://github.com/search?q=instagram+story+upload&type=repositories&s=updated)), and no Appium or Playwright Story-posting project turned up either ([GitHub search](https://github.com/search?q=instagram+appium+story&type=repositories)).

### Login, not uploading, is where the account is at risk

The weak point in October 2026 is authentication. instagrapi now logs in through Instagram's new "CAA" (Bloks) flow ([README](https://github.com/subzeroid/instagrapi)), and open issues report three problems with it. In #2890, a successful login is followed by `challenge_required` and an endless "approve this login" loop, and the reporter's other accounts were suspended until phone-and-selfie verification ([issue #2890](https://github.com/subzeroid/instagrapi/issues/2890)). In #2881, TOTP logins return no session and fall back to `needs_upgrade` ([issue #2881](https://github.com/subzeroid/instagrapi/issues/2881)). Others report "Your version of Instagram is out of date" ([issue #2807](https://github.com/subzeroid/instagrapi/issues/2807)). Library upgrades have also silently changed a saved session's device fingerprint ([issue #2873](https://github.com/subzeroid/instagrapi/issues/2873)). The maintainer's own advice is to **reuse one saved session** via `dump_settings()`/`load_settings()`, keep "one account per stable proxy/IP", avoid repeated fresh password logins, and treat challenges as something the library cannot reliably bypass ([best-practices.md](https://github.com/subzeroid/instagrapi/blob/master/docs/usage-guide/best-practices.md)). Since 3.0.24, `Client(with_challenge_flow=False)` raises challenges to the caller instead of blocking on stdin, which matters for a headless NAS ([CHANGELOG](https://github.com/subzeroid/instagrapi/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md)). Instagram's terms prohibit unauthorized automated access ([Instagram Terms](https://help.instagram.com/581066165581870)), so this path is always at the owner's risk. The README itself calls private-API automation "fragile in production" ([README](https://github.com/subzeroid/instagrapi)).

Two caveats are untested. No 2025–2026 source confirms that `audience: besties` still works for **video** Stories, though none reports it broken either. The private API's server-side duration cap is also unconfirmed. Titler should verify both with one test post and a check for the green ring in the app.

## Telegram's user API covers every audience, but free-tier quotas block a series

Telegram reverses Instagram's split: the official bot route is the limited one. **Bot API `postStory`** (added in 9.0, April 2025) posts only "on behalf of a managed business account" with the `can_manage_stories` right. Its parameters include `active_period`, caption, areas and `protect_content`, but **no privacy field** ([Bot API](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#poststory)). Connecting a business bot doesn't require Premium ([Telegram Business API](https://core.telegram.org/api/business)). Close friends, contacts or selected users can't be targeted this way, and no Bot API method posts Stories to a channel ([Bot API changelog](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api-changelog)).

The **MTProto user API** is the real tool. `stories.sendStory` with `peer=inputPeerSelf` takes a required `privacy_rules` vector plus `pinned`, `noforwards`, caption, media areas, albums and `period` ([stories.sendStory](https://core.telegram.org/method/stories.sendStory)). The privacy constructors include `AllowAll`, `AllowContacts`, `AllowCloseFriends`, `AllowUsers{users}`, `DisallowUsers`, `AllowChatParticipants` and others. The close-friends list is replaced wholesale with `contacts.editCloseFriends`, and **only existing contacts can be added** ([Telegram: Privacy](https://core.telegram.org/api/privacy)). Telegram's API Terms allow this with your own `api_id`. They forbid acting without the user's knowledge, and breaches get a 10-day notice before API access is cut ([API Terms](https://core.telegram.org/api/terms)).

Quotas are the real blocker. Telegram's documented config defaults give free accounts **1 active story, 3 per week, 10 per month, 200-character captions and a 24 h period only**. Premium gets 100 active, 700 per week, 3000 per month, 2048-character captions and 6/12/24/48 h periods ([Client configuration](https://core.telegram.org/api/config)). Breaching them returns `STORIES_TOO_MUCH` or `STORY_SEND_FLOOD_WEEKLY_%d` ([Stories API](https://core.telegram.org/api/stories)). Those values are the documentation's sample, and real values are per-account. Titler should read `help.getAppConfig` and call `stories.canSendStory` before each upload, since "limit conditions can change between canSendStory and sendStory" ([Stories API](https://core.telegram.org/api/stories)). If the defaults hold, a free account can't post part 2 while part 1 is live, so **any multi-part series needs Telegram Premium**.

Video specs are tighter than Instagram's. The Bot API and TDLib specify **720×1280, H.265, a keyframe every second, streamable MPEG4, at most 30 MB, at most 60 s** ([Bot API](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#inputstorycontentvideo); [TDLib](https://core.telegram.org/tdlib/docs/classtd_1_1td__api_1_1input_story_content_video.html)). MTProto states only "vertical video, up to 30 MB" ([Stories API](https://core.telegram.org/api/stories)). A 59 s part must therefore average under about 4 Mbit/s. Titler should render a separate Telegram encode (720×1280 HEVC, `-g` equal to the fps, faststart, about 3 Mbit/s video) rather than reuse the Instagram master. Nobody has tested whether MTProto rejects 1080p H.264.

| MadelineProto | PHP | Generic raw methods | 8.13.2 on 2026-10-26 ([GitHub](https://github.com/danog/MadelineProto)) |
| Pyrogram, Pyrofork, GramJS | Python / Node | Had helpers | Archived ([Pyrogram](https://github.com/pyrogram/pyrogram), [Pyrofork](https://github.com/Mayuri-Chan/pyrofork), [GramJS](https://github.com/gram-js/gramjs)) |

Kurigram's docstring notes that `disallowed_users` works only with PUBLIC and CONTACTS ([send_story.py](https://github.com/kurigram-org/kurigram/blob/HEAD/pyrogram/methods/stories/send_story.py)). There is no batch call and no documented ordering guarantee, so a series is posted sequentially, waiting for each `updateStoryID` before the next ([Stories API](https://core.telegram.org/api/stories)). Albums can group the parts on the profile after they expire. The session file grants full account access, so it needs `chmod 600` and an encrypted backup. Third-party FAQs say unofficial-client accounts are "placed under observation" and that ban criteria are opaque ([tgsnake FAQ](https://tgsnake.js.org/en/faq)). A few self-posts a day is a low-risk use.

## Titler should add a two-adapter publish stage with a manual fallback

The table below maps each requirement to the best route.

| Goal | Route | Account requirement | Automation level |
|---|---|---|---|
| IG public Story | Graph API `STORIES` via signed `video_url` | Business (Creator unverified) | Full, official |
| IG Close Friends Story | instagrapi `audience: besties` | Any, including personal | Full, unofficial, ToS risk |
| IG fallback | Phone handoff, Web Share or notification | Any | Manual tap |
| TG Story, any audience | Kurigram `send_story` (MTProto) | Personal; Premium for series | Full, permitted |
| TG via bot | Bot API `postStory` | Business account | Full, public/default audience only |

In practice, Titler's export screen gains a "Publish" action that queues the parts on n5. Each platform gets its own worker process, and each runs strictly sequentially. The **Telegram worker** uses Kurigram with a one-time interactive login, the owner's own `api_id`, a pre-encoded 720×1280 HEVC file, `privacy=CLOSE_FRIENDS` (or contacts/selected users from a per-series setting), and a `canSendStory` check before every part. The **Instagram worker** uses instagrapi with a pinned version, one session saved by `dump_settings` after an interactive first login on the home network, `with_challenge_flow=False`, no proxy (n5's residential IP is the stable identity), and `audience: besties` when the series is marked Close Friends. Both workers stop the queue and send the owner a Telegram alert on `ChallengeRequired`, `LoginRequired`, `FeedbackRequired` or a flood error, instead of retrying. If the series is public and the account is Business, the Graph API adapter is the lower-risk choice and should be preferred. Every queued item should also expose a "send to my phone" fallback (download plus Web Share), so a broken login never strands a series.

Two n5-specific risks deserve mention. n5 sits at a fixed home location, so when the owner travels Instagram sees the server and the phone in different places. The private-API notes flag that as the main trigger for "suspicious login" prompts. A dynamic ISP lease can also change the IP under a saved session. Neither blocks the plan, but both argue for alerting over silent retries.

## Conclusion

Close Friends is the requirement that decides the architecture. Without it, both platforms have clean official or permitted paths. With it, Telegram stays clean because MTProto user sessions are a sanctioned API, while Instagram falls back to a reverse-engineered client whose login flow changes every few weeks. The cost is ongoing maintenance on the Instagram side: pinning versions, watching instagrapi's issue tracker after Instagram app updates, and accepting occasional manual re-approval. Two cheap upfront actions would remove most of the remaining uncertainty: one test Close Friends video Story through instagrapi from n5, and a check of `stories.canSendStory` on the owner's Telegram account to see whether the free-tier quota (and so the case for Premium) applies.
